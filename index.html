ProperPDF

Your documents.
Your device. Your business.

I created ProperPDF, an offline-first PDF utility with fully native Android and iOS apps for working with documents directly on your device.

The product decision
Keep document processing on the device. Everyday PDF tasks should work without uploading private files or depending on a connection.
The shipped product
Merge, split, compress, sign, and organize PDFs in one app. Available on Android and iOS.

  • Offline-first

02 / Independent product · Native iOS

ProperImage

Everyday image tools.
Processing stays on your device.

I created ProperImage, a native iOS image toolkit for iPhone and iPad. It brings conversion, resizing, compression, and background removal into one app.

The product decision
Process images locally, so working with your photos does not require uploading them to a server.
The shipped product
Convert HEIC to JPG, work with multiple photos at once, create image PDFs, and prepare images for sharing.
  • Native iOS
  • iPhone & iPad
  • On-device processing
  • Batch workflows
